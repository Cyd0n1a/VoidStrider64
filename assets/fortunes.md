## 🧠 General Trivia

71. Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still edible.

72. A group of flamingos is called a flamboyance.

73. Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Teaching began there around 1096; the Aztec Empire was founded in 1428.

74. Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.

75. Bananas are technically berries, but strawberries are not.

76. The average cloud weighs around 1.1 million pounds.

77. A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus — it rotates so slowly that it completes one orbit of the Sun before finishing one full spin.

78. The human eye can distinguish about 10 million different colours.

79. Sharks are older than trees. Sharks have existed for around 450 million years; trees appeared about 350 million years ago.

80. There are more possible iterations of a game of chess than there are atoms in the observable universe.

81. The dot above the letters "i" and "j" is called a tittle.

82. Scotland's national animal is the unicorn.

83. It would take about 1.2 million mosquitoes, each sucking once, to drain the average human of all their blood.

84. A jiffy is an actual unit of time: 1/100th of a second in computing.

85. Wombat faeces are cube-shaped — the only known animal to produce cubic poop.

86. The word "nerd" was first coined by Dr. Seuss in the 1950 book *If I Ran the Zoo*.

87. There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on all of Earth's beaches combined.

88. Crows can recognise and remember human faces, and will hold grudges against people who have wronged them.

89. The Eiffel Tower grows about 15 cm taller in summer due to thermal expansion of the iron.

90. The longest recorded flight of a chicken is 13 seconds.

91. Hot water can freeze faster than cold water under certain conditions — this is called the Mpemba effect.

92. A "group of porcupines" is called a prickle.

93. The tongue is the only muscle in the human body attached at only one end.

94. Maine is the closest US state to Africa.

95. Octopuses have three hearts, blue blood, and nine brains (one central brain and one in each arm).

96. The fingerprints of a koala are so similar to human fingerprints that they have been mistaken for human prints at crime scenes.

97. Bees can recognise human faces using the same method humans do, by piecing together individual features.

98. The average person walks about 100,000 miles in their lifetime — roughly four laps around the equator.

99. There are more atoms in a single glass of water than there are glasses of water in all of Earth's oceans.

100. Nintendo was founded in 1889 — originally as a playing card company.
